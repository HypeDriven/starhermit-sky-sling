How to play

Aim

Drag back from the slingshot pouch — pull down and left to send the shot up and to the right. The dotted arc previews your shot.

Launch

Release or press Space to launch. Each launch uses one shot.

Collapse

Knock out supports; falling blocks crush the green Bumbles. Clear every target before shots run out.

Materials

Ice (pale, smooth) breaks easily, wood (brown) is sturdy, stone (grey) is tough.

Keyboard

←→↑↓ aim, Space launch, P/Esc pause, R restart, U undo in practice, S fast-forward.

Score

Targets 1000 each, blocks by material, plus 500 per unused shot. Beat par for stars.
